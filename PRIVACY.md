# Focusaurus privacy policy

Focusaurus works in your browser profile. We do not operate a server that receives your browsing data, and the extension contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Privacy policy · Effective September 13, 2026 · Version 1.2

## What the extension stores

Your chosen sites and paths, work-hours schedule, preferences, dinosaur name, current session, and temporary passes are stored locally using your browser’s extension storage. History contains daily counts of blocked attempts and temporary passes for each configured site, plus session start and end times.

Focusaurus does not measure time spent on websites or read page content. It does not keep a log of every page you visit. When a site is blocked, its original URL appears in the blocked page’s address so that you can return to it. That address may be visible in your browser’s own history or history sync, which are controlled by your browser.

## Why access to websites is requested

Your browser needs website access to redirect sites you choose during a focus session. Rules apply to top-level HTTP and HTTPS navigations. Focusaurus checks open tab addresses when blocking changes and uses navigation events to catch route changes inside sites that do not reload the page. Those addresses are processed locally and are not added to a browsing log. It does not inject scripts into websites, inspect forms, or read passwords. It uses the storage permission for your settings and history, and alarms for session timing and scheduled work hours.

## What leaves your browser

The extension does not send settings or usage history to Focusaurus, advertisers, or other services. Its fonts, illustrations, and code are bundled. No account is required. Settings are not automatically synchronized across devices.

If you export settings, you choose where to save or share that file. The file contains your configured sites and preferences, but no attempt history. Your browser’s extension distribution, browser services, and the websites you visit have their own privacy practices.

## Retention and deletion

History keeps today and the previous six local calendar days. Older records are deleted when Focusaurus starts, when you open its controls, and during periodic maintenance while your browser is running. Settings remain until you replace them or uninstall the extension. Choose “Clear history” in Settings to delete recorded history while keeping your current configuration and session. Uninstalling Focusaurus removes its extension storage from that browser profile. Exported files and your browser’s own browsing history must be deleted separately.

## Upgrading from a prototype

Earlier Focusaurus versions stored settings in Chrome sync. Version 1.0 copies those settings into local storage, then removes the old Focusaurus sync keys after the copy succeeds. It does not synchronize subsequent changes. Chrome controls processing of previously synced data.

## Use of data

Locally processed information is used only to provide Focusaurus’s focus sessions, blocking, preferences, and history features. It is not sold, used for advertising, transferred to data brokers, or used to determine creditworthiness. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

## Questions and changes

Use the publisher contact shown on Focusaurus’s Chrome Web Store or Firefox Add-ons listing for privacy questions. Changes to this policy will be included with the extension and reflected in the effective date above.
