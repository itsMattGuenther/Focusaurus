Bundled fonts
=============

Both typefaces below are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1,
which permits bundling and redistribution inside this extension.

Fraunces
  Display / headline face. Variable, with optical-size (opsz) and weight axes.
  Copyright (c) The Fraunces Project Authors
  https://github.com/undercasetype/Fraunces
  Latin subset only.

Public Sans
  Body and UI face. Variable weight axis.
  Copyright (c) The Public Sans Project Authors
  https://github.com/uswds/public-sans
  Latin subset only.

Full license text: https://openfontlicense.org/

Why these are vendored rather than linked
-----------------------------------------
An extension's Content Security Policy forbids loading resources from remote
hosts, and Focusaurus makes no network requests by design (see docs/DESIGN.md
§7). Both files are the latin subset only, ~94KB combined.
